// The Story

Who I Am

I've spent around a decade in Quality across regulated manufacturing: Apple Operations Europe, Johnson & Johnson MedTech, and now as a Senior Quality Technician at Stryker Neurovascular in Cork. My day job is making sure life-critical devices are built right, traceably, and to standard.

Seeing how completely safety-critical production now depends on digital systems, and how much a security failure could cost a regulated line, turned a long-standing interest into a direction. I went back to study formally: a BSc in Network Cybersecurity (Distinction), and now an MSc in Cybersecurity, Privacy & Trust, all while working full-time rotating shifts.

The thread through both careers is the same: controlled processes, evidence, and risk managed rather than hoped away. I'm most interested in where regulated manufacturing meets security: the OT/ICS and enterprise systems a plant depends on, and in GRC work where my audit-and-compliance background is an advantage, not a retread.

"The best security control is the one you can prove was working. That's a Quality mindset, and it's exactly what good GRC needs."
