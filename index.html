// The Angle

Regulated-industry discipline, applied to security

A decade in Quality across Apple, Johnson & Johnson MedTech and Stryker Neurovascular taught me how safety-critical systems really fail — and how auditable controls, traceability and risk management keep them standing.

That foundation maps directly onto cybersecurity governance. ISO 13485 and 21 CFR Part 820 are close cousins of ISO 27001 and NIST CSF; a CAPA is an incident response process by another name. My work focuses where those worlds meet: securing the OT/ICS and enterprise systems that run regulated manufacturing.
