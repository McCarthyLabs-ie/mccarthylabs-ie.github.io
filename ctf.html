How I practise

Every offensive exercise serves a defensive end. I run a technique only against equipment I own, on an isolated network, and the write-up always lands on the same question: what would have caught this, and what would have stopped it? A finding without a fix is half a lesson.

That discipline comes straight from regulated-manufacturing Quality: reproduce the failure, record the evidence, close it out with a control you can prove works. It's the same loop, pointed at security.
